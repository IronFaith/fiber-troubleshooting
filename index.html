Reading the optic health report
  • RX is received light; TX is reported outgoing light. Check both active ends and each affected lane.
  • Test the optics before tracing the production fiber. Start the VFL at the disconnected active-end fiber, then follow the corresponding passive ports.
  • Use the model’s actual RX/TX specifications and current readings. A −40 display may be a reporting floor; its meaning depends on the device. A green badge or a positive TX value alone does not establish health.
  • VFL shows continuity and routing. A power meter or loss test is needed to establish acceptable optical loss.
