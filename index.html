RX is low. Where is the light?

Start with RX −40 or a below-limit reading.
Follow each branch to narrow down the cause.

Far-end TX→Fiber & connections→Local RX

Local = the end with low RX. Check one direction and its affected lanes at a time. If both ends are low, repeat for the other direction.

Reading the optic health report
  • RX is received light; TX is the optic’s reported outgoing light. Pair local RX with the far-end TX lane that feeds it.
  • Record which lanes are affected. All lanes at −40 and one low lane need different attention to the common route versus an individual lane.
  • Use the model’s actual RX/TX specifications and current readings. A −40 display may be a reporting floor; its meaning depends on the device. A green badge or a positive TX value alone does not establish health.
  • A “No neighbor” result does not identify whether the cause is fiber, an optic, or configuration.
